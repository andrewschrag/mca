Where the Data Lives — Scoring Explorer

Patient Data Initiative · seven data-custody options · edit any score, mark or weight and the ranking recomputes

MCA is building one platform under every option here: a CHD knowledge graph, AI extraction over clinical notes, assembly of datasets against definitions a user supplies, and governance carried on each record. That stack does not change with the choice below. What changes is where the identifiable record comes to rest, and who holds the identifiers that turn scattered encounters into one longitudinal history.

That boundary sets everything downstream — which agreements MCA signs, whether MCA becomes a HIPAA business associate, which of the fourteen intended uses are reachable at all, and what MCA can promise a hospital. The build does not wait on it. The legal workstream cannot start without it, and paper signed under one custody assumption is expensive to unwind under another.

So these are seven answers to one question, not seven architectures. Release policy, where computation runs, and how much federation to absorb are settings inside whichever answer is chosen.
